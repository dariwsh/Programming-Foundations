## 🧠 Interactive Quiz

**Question:** What is the bitwise AND operation (5 & 3) result?

<details>
<summary><b>Option A:</b> 1</summary>

✅ <b>Correct!</b> Correct! Binary 5 is 101, and 3 is 011. 101 & 011 is 001, which is 1 in decimal.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Option B:</b> 7</summary>

❌ <b>Incorrect!</b> 7 is the result of a bitwise OR operation (5 | 3).
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Option C:</b> 8</summary>

❌ <b>Incorrect!</b> 8 is the addition result, not bitwise AND.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Option D:</b> 0</summary>

❌ <b>Incorrect!</b> Since 5 and 3 share a set bit at position 0, the result is not zero.
</details>
