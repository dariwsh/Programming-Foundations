## 🧠 Interactive Quiz

**Question:** What is the bitwise OR operation (5 | 3) result?

<details>
<summary><b>Option A:</b> 1</summary>

❌ <b>Incorrect!</b> 1 is the bitwise AND result (5 & 3).
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Option B:</b> 7</summary>

✅ <b>Correct!</b> Correct! Binary 5 is 101, and 3 is 011. 101 | 011 is 111, which is 7 in decimal.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Option C:</b> 8</summary>

❌ <b>Incorrect!</b> 8 is addition, not bitwise OR.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Option D:</b> 15</summary>

❌ <b>Incorrect!</b> 15 is the result of binary 1111, not 5 | 3.
</details>
