---

## 🏫 Lecture Notes (The Doctor's Way)

A **Metropolitan Area Network (MAN)** is a computer network that covers a larger geographic area than a LAN but a smaller area than a WAN—typically a city or a large university campus.
*   **Scale:** Typically ranges from 5 to 50 kilometers.
*   **Ownership:** Often owned by a consortium of users or a single network provider who sells the service to users (like cable television networks or municipal government networks).
*   **Speed & Media:** MANs usually use optical fiber cables to achieve high-speed communication between multiple LANs.
