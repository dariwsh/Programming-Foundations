## 🏫 Lecture Notes (The Doctor's Way)

A **Domain Name** is an easy-to-remember alias for an IP address.
*   **Hierarchy:**
    *   *TLD (Top-Level Domain):* The extension at the end (e.g., `.com`, `.org`, `.net`, `.edu`).
    *   *SLD (Second-Level Domain):* The unique name selected by the registrant (e.g., `google` in `google.com`).
*   **Acquisition:** Domains cannot be purchased permanently. They are leased through **Registrars** (e.g., GoDaddy, Namecheap) certified by **ICANN** (Internet Corporation for Assigned Names and Numbers).

   * B) org

   * D) Neither

2. **What organization globally coordinates the assignment of domain names and IP addresses?**
   * A) IEEE
   * B) W3C
   * C) ICANN
   * D) NASA

3. **How do you secure a domain name for your website?**
   * A) You buy it forever from the government.
   * B) You lease it from a domain registrar for a recurring fee.
   * C) You program it into your router.
   * D) Domains are free for anyone to write.
