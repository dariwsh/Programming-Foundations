## 🏫 Lecture Notes (The Doctor's Way)

An **ISP (Internet Service Provider)** is a company that provides access to the Internet. The global ISP architecture is structured in three tiers:
*   **Tier 1 ISPs:** The backbone of the global internet. They own transatlantic/intercontinental fiber lines and connect directly to other Tier 1 providers without paying for transit (peering).
*   **Tier 2 ISPs:** Regional providers. They pay Tier 1 ISPs for internet access but may peer with each other to reduce costs.
*   **Tier 3 ISPs:** Retail providers (local companies) that sell internet connections directly to residential consumers and small businesses.
