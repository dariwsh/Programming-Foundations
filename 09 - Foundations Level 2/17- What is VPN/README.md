## 🏫 Lecture Notes (The Doctor's Way)

A **VPN (Virtual Private Network)** secures and anonymizes your network connection.
*   **How it Works:** It establishes an encrypted tunnel between your device and a secure remote VPN server.
*   **Process:**
    1.  Your data is encrypted by the VPN software on your device.
    2.  It travels securely through your ISP's lines.
    3.  It reaches the VPN server, which decrypts it.
    4.  The VPN server forwards the request with its own public IP, hiding your identity.
*   **Benefits:** Prevents ISP snooping, bypasses geo-restrictions, and secures public Wi-Fi traffic from eavesdropping.

## 📝 Self-Assessment Quiz

### Questions
1. **What is the primary method a VPN uses to protect data from being snooped by ISPs or hackers?**
   * A) Deleting browsing history.
   * B) Compressing data packets.
   * C) Encrypting the data within a secure tunnel.
   * D) Speeding up network cables.

2. **Whose IP address does a website see when you access it while connected to a VPN?**
   * A) Your local private IP
   * B) Your home router's public IP
   * C) The VPN server's public IP
   * D) Your ISP's gateway IP

3. **Why is a VPN recommended when using public Wi-Fi networks (like in coffee shops)?**
   * A) It makes the coffee shop's internet connection faster.
   * B) It blocks physical observers from looking at your screen.
   * C) It encrypts local traffic, preventing malicious actors on the same network from intercepting your data.
   * D) It charges your device's battery.

### Answer Key
1. **C)**
   * *Explanation:* Encryption ensures that even if traffic is captured, it cannot be read without the decryption keys.
2. **C)**
   * *Explanation:* The VPN server acts as a proxy, forwarding your traffic so websites only see the VPN's IP.
3. **C)**
   * *Explanation:* Public Wi-Fi is often unencrypted, making it easy for interceptors on the same local network to sniff packets. A VPN prevents this via end-to-end encryption.
